The model is prepared explicitly with python -m backend.vision.model --download.
Images are processed on the camera device. Optional preview is transient and
enabled explicitly; it is not a recording or cloud inference service.
The geometric posture rules are FocusGuard prototype heuristics and do not
establish clinical accuracy or an assessment of attention.
